FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents need to know about our classes, syllabus, and enrollment.

What is the maximum number of students per class?
We strictly limit physical classes to a maximum of 12 students to guarantee individualized guidance and direct tutor attention.
Which syllabus does Pusat Tuisyen Bestari Didik follow?
Our curriculum is 100% aligned with the latest Ministry of Education (MOE) KSSR Semakan and KSSM syllabuses, as well as Cambridge IGCSE.
How do parents track student progress?
Diagnostic Assessment Tests are conducted every 3 months with a detailed report card issued directly to parents.
Can students switch between physical and online classes?
Yes! Our modular curriculum is synchronized so students can easily attend live online sessions if unable to visit the centre.
